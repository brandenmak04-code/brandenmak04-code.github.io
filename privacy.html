NLB Booker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

NLB Booker is a personal Telegram bot used only by its owner, Branden Mak. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google data the app uses

The app requests two Google scopes:

  • Google Calendar events: to add and remove events for seat bookings on the owner's calendar.
  • Google Sheets: to read and delete rows in one sheet the owner uses to pass Telegram messages to the bot.

Storage and sharing

The Google sign-in token and all data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI training.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
